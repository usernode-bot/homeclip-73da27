Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to HomeClip, a place for a homeroom to follow the serial dramas it watches together. These terms apply when you use the app.

Using the app

HomeClip is a shared catalogue, not a video host. The group adds the dramas and episodes it watches, follows the shows it likes, and marks episodes watched. HomeClip plays no video of its own: an episode can carry a link, which Watch opens in a new tab, and an episode's owner can attach a video link that plays inside the app.

Your account

You sign in with your Homeroom account. Do not use the app to break the law or anyone's rights, and do not add content you have no right to share.

What you add

Dramas, episodes, links, subtitles links and reports you add stay in the app's own database. You are responsible for what you add. Only the person who added a drama or an episode can remove it; reports are recorded for the group's admins and nothing is hidden automatically.

Downloads

Saving an episode for offline watching stores it in your own browser on your own device, only if the episode's video source allows it. Nothing is stored on HomeClip's servers, and downloads are not shared with anyone else.
